# GApps Integration for AlbOS

AlbOS includes Google Apps pre-integrated in the ROM build.

## Included Google Apps

- Gmail
- Google Maps
- Google Play Store
- YouTube
- Google Chrome
- Google Drive
- Google Photos
- Google Authenticator

## Installation

GApps se instaleaza automat la flashare. Nu trebuie sa faci nimic special.

## Configuration

Toti Google Services sunt preconfigurati:
- Play Services
- Google Play Framework
- Google Account Management

## Permissions

Totele permisiunile necesare pentru Google Apps sunt incluse in manifest.
